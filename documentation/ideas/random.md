A family of services under `puck.uno/random/*` that produce random values on request. Examples:

- `puck.uno/random/digit` — a random digit
- `puck.uno/random/uuid` — a random UUID
- `puck.uno/random/geolocation` — a random latitude/longitude (possibly constrained to a region)
- `puck.uno/random/data` — random data of some shape

Not designed yet — just noting the family so the namespace and the intent are recorded. Revisit when there's a concrete use case driving the first one.
